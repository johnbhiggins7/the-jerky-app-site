Common questions

A few quick answers.

How do I sign in?

Open Profile in the iPhone app and choose an available sign-in option. Apple and Google sign-in are supported in the current testing build. Google access may be limited to approved testers while its consent screen is in Testing.

Will signing in upload my guest shelf?

No. Signing in does not upload guest records. Account sync is a separate choice in Profile.

How do I back up a private package photo?

Turn on private photo backup for your account, then choose Upload private photo for that package. Each photo requires its own upload action.

How do I submit my brand?

Sign in, open Profile → Submit your brand, and provide your official website and business contact details. You can return to that screen to see your submission status. Submitting a brand does not automatically publish it.

How do I delete my account?

Open Profile → Delete account in the app. If you cannot access your account, email us from the address associated with it so we can help verify your request.
