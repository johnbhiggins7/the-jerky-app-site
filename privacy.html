Privacy policy

Your shelf is personal. This page explains what stays on your iPhone, what you can choose to back up, and what happens when you sign in.

Effective September 29, 2026

Overview

The Jerky App is an iPhone catalog and personal field guide. You can browse as a guest. Guest shelves, tastings, notes, private packages, and preferences stay on your device. Signing in alone does not upload those records. Account sync and private photo backup are separate choices.

This policy covers the iPhone app and this website. The Jerky App is operated by John Higgins. Questions can be sent to johnbhiggins7@gmail.com.

Data we handle

Account and sign-in

If you sign in, our authentication provider, Supabase, processes your account identifier, email address, provider identity, and session information. Sign in with Apple or Google also involves that provider under its own privacy terms. For Google sign-in, the app requests basic identity scopes only: openid, email, and profile. We use them to identify your account; we do not request access to your Google Drive, contacts, or Gmail. Session credentials are stored in the iPhone Keychain.

Personal field notes

The app stores your shelves, notes, tastings, preferences, and private package records locally. If you enable account sync, those account records are sent to Supabase so they can be restored and used across your signed-in devices. Guest records are not uploaded by signing in.

Photos and camera

If you add a private package photo, the photo is stored on your device. Private photo backup is off by default. Turning it on does not upload existing photos: each photo needs a separate Upload private photo action. Uploaded photos and related file metadata are stored in a private Supabase Storage bucket for your account. Camera and photo-library access are used only when you choose a related app feature. Public product images, when available, may load from the maker or its image host; that host receives the image request and network address.

Brand submissions and support

A signed-in brand representative can submit a brand name, official website, contact name and email, role, optional product link and note, and an authority attestation. This information goes to a private review inbox and is not automatically published. If you email support, we receive your address and the details you choose to send.

Technical data

Supabase, Apple, Google, image hosts, and GitHub Pages may process network addresses, device or browser information, and operational logs when their services are used. The app has no advertising tracker or third-party analytics SDK. Its local diagnostics are in memory and are not uploaded as analytics.

How we use information

We use information to authenticate you, provide the catalog and your optional account features, keep private backups available to your account, review brand submissions, respond to support requests, secure the service, and diagnose service failures. We do not sell your personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

Services and sharing

Supabase hosts authentication, optional account records, private photo backup, and private brand submissions. Apple and Google handle sign-in when you choose them. GitHub hosts these public pages. Each service may handle operational data under its own terms. Opening a maker link takes you to that maker’s website, where its privacy practices apply. We may disclose information when required by law or to protect the service and its users.

Your choices

  • Use the app as a guest and keep personal records on your device.
  • Sign in and separately enable or disable account sync and private photo backup.
  • Choose which private photos to upload, restore, or remove.
  • Manage camera and photo-library access in iPhone Settings.
  • Use Profile → Delete account in the app to request deletion of your account and cloud data, or contact support if you cannot access the app.

Signing out locks that account’s local records on the device until you sign in again. Guest records remain available.

Retention and deletion

Account data and private uploaded photos remain while your account is active, unless you remove them sooner. Account deletion removes the account’s cloud records and stored private photo files and clears that account’s records from the device after deletion completes. Guest records remain on the device until you remove them or the app’s local data is erased. Offline devices cannot be remotely erased while disconnected. Service providers may retain limited operational records under their own policies or legal obligations. Brand submissions are deleted with the submitting account; independently documented public catalog evidence may remain if a brand was reviewed and published.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. We will post the new version here with a revised effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions or a deletion request you cannot complete in the app, email johnbhiggins7@gmail.com. You can also visit Support.
